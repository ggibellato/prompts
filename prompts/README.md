# Prompts

Standalone, ready-to-use prompts.

- [text-review.md](text-review.md) - Corrects grammar and spelling and improves clarity, cohesion, and flow while preserving the original meaning and tone.
- [technical-second-option.md](technical-second-option.md) - Acts as a senior engineer giving a second opinion on a technical (authentication) decision: challenges assumptions, flags risks and trade-offs, and recommends the safest option.
